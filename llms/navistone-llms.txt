# NaviStone

> NaviStone is a direct-mail marketing technology company that turns first-party and "unknown" website audiences into addressable postcard campaigns for consumer brands. Its platform API manages clients, domains, audience segments, campaigns (linked to Modern Postcard creative), ZIP/state geo-targeting, and output tracking, secured with an X-API-Key header.
